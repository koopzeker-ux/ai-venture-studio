# Opportunity Strategy

The system searches through multiple lenses:

1. Pain discovery — recurring expensive problems and complaints.
2. Trend discovery — accelerating demand and new behavior.
3. Winner discovery — products/businesses already showing traction.
4. Market gaps — validated demand with weak, expensive or poorly localized solutions.

Successful existing products are studied for economic logic, customer demand and gaps; protected code, branding, creative assets and other IP are not copied.
