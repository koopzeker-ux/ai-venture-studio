# Principles

- Validate before building.
- Measure before scaling.
- Kill weak ideas quickly.
- Scale winners when evidence is strong.
- Separate facts, assumptions and hypotheses.
- Use independent verification where material.
- Least privilege for agents and tools.
- Human approval for material spend, legal commitments, DNS, critical secrets, destructive actions and risky production changes.
- No AI theater: every recurring job must justify its cost.
